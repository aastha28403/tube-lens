YouTube Chat

Chat with any YouTube video

How to get the video ID or URL:
1. Open any YouTube video in your browser.
2. Copy the URL from the address bar — e.g.
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ
3. Paste the full URL or just the 11-character code after v=.

Also works with: youtu.be/ short links & /shorts/ URLs.
Note: The video must have captions (CC) enabled.
